## 0.1.0

- `sortedArray()` factory with binary search insert and lookup

- `range()` queries for retrieving subsets
- Custom comparator support
- Iterable interface with `Symbol.iterator`
- `length`, `first`, and `last` getters
- `toArray()` for safe copy
